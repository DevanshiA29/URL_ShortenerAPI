# Module 05 Context: Stakeholder Communication & Translation

## Micro-Exercise: Explaining Technical Concepts in Business & Plain Language

### Part 1: Explaining "Database Migration" to a Bakery Owner
"Imagine your bakery keeps every recipe, wedding cake order, customer allergy note, and ingredient supplier phone number in an old three-ring binder behind the cash register. A database migration is like carefully copying all those recipes and orders into a new, color-coded, indexed filing system. While we are physically moving and verifying the recipe cards after closing hours, the kitchen cannot accept new orders for two hours so nobody bakes with half an order."

### Part 2: Explaining WHY to Do It (The Business Case)
"Why do it? Because right now, during the Saturday morning rush, your bakers spend 3 minutes flipping through dog-eared pages just to verify one customer's nut allergy or check cupcake inventory. Customers wait in line, some walk out, and mistakes happen. With the new system, your staff finds any recipe or custom cake order in 3 seconds. Lines move 40% faster, order mix-ups drop to zero, and your bakery can handle 500 orders a day instead of capping out at 200 without having to hire extra staff."
